## 1. What you need

- A device: Amazon Fire TV Stick, Android TV box, Nvidia Shield, MAG box, Smart TV, phone or computer.
- An IPTV player app (TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro or similar).
- A subscription login from your provider: either an **Xtream Codes** login (server URL, username, password) or an **M3U playlist URL**.

## 2. Check your internet first

Most buffering problems come from the home network, not the provider.

| Quality | Recommended stable speed |
|---|---|
| SD / HD | 15 Mbps |
| Full HD / 4K | 25 Mbps or more |

- Test at [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net) at the time you usually watch (evenings and weekends).
- Use an Ethernet cable if your device has a port. If it doesn't, use the 5 GHz Wi-Fi band and sit close to the router.
- Restart your router if speeds are far below your plan.

## 3. Set up on Firestick or Android TV with TiviMate

### Step 1: Install Downloader and allow app installs (Firestick)
1. Search for **Downloader** in the Amazon Appstore and install it.
2. Go to **Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options**, open **Install unknown apps** (called *Apps from Unknown Sources* on older models), and turn it on for **Downloader**.
   - If you don't see Developer Options, go to **Settings > My Fire TV > About** and click your device name 7 times.

### Step 2: Install the player
- **Android TV / Google TV:** install **TiviMate** from Google Play.
- **Firestick:** open Downloader and enter the download link published on TiviMate's official website. Only use the official link, not a mirror.

### Step 3: Add your playlist
1. Open TiviMate and choose **Add Playlist**.
2. Choose **Xtream Codes** and enter the server URL, username and password from your provider's email. If you only have an M3U link, choose **M3U Playlist**.
3. Let the channel list and TV guide (EPG) finish loading before you start browsing.

### Step 4: Recommended settings
- Turn on automatic **EPG updates**.
- If your device is old, lower the default stream quality or use a smaller buffer so channels start quickly.

## 4. Set up on IPTV Smarters or a Smart TV

1. Install **IPTV Smarters Pro** (or another player available in your TV's app store).
2. Choose **Login with Xtream Codes API**.
3. Enter any name, then your username, password and server URL.
4. Tap **Add User** and wait for the list to download.

On Samsung and LG Smart TVs, use a player app from the TV's own store that supports Xtream Codes or M3U.

## 5. Fix buffering and freezing

Work through this list in order:

1. Run a speed test at the time the problem happens.
2. Move to Ethernet or the 5 GHz Wi-Fi band.
3. Restart the router and the streaming device.
4. Close other heavy downloads or streams on the network.
5. Clear the player app's cache, then reload the playlist.
6. Try a different channel to see if the problem is one channel or all of them.
7. Contact your provider with the time, channel and what you saw.

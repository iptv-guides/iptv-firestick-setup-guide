# How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung, LG and Android Smart TVs (2026)

> **Disclosure:** Written by the Ark IPTV team. Steps apply to any Xtream Codes or M3U provider. Back to the [main setup guide](README.md).
>
> *Last updated: October 2026*

## Before you start
- Your login from the provider: Xtream Codes (server URL, username, password) or an M3U link. See [Xtream Codes vs M3U](xtream-codes-vs-m3u.md).
- Your TV connected to the internet, ideally by Ethernet.

## Android TV and Google TV (Sony, TCL, Philips and others)
1. Open the Google Play Store on the TV.
2. Install an IPTV player such as IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate.
3. Open it and choose Xtream Codes (or M3U).
4. Enter the details, save, and wait for channels and the guide to load.

## Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS)
Availability of apps changes by model year and region, so check your TV's own app store.
1. Search the store for an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U.
2. Install it and open it.
3. Add your playlist with the details from your provider.
4. If no suitable app exists for your model, use a Firestick or Android TV box instead.

## Tips
- Name your playlist so you can find it later.
- Turn on EPG updates in the app.
- If it buffers, follow the [buffering fix list](iptv-buffering-fix.md).

# How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung, LG and Android Smart TVs (2026)

> **Disclosure:** Written by the Ark IPTV support team. Steps apply to any Xtream Codes or M3U provider. Back to the [main setup guide](README.md).
>
> *Last updated: October 2026*

## The short answer

On an **Android TV or Google TV** (Sony, TCL, Philips, Hisense Google TV models and others), install TiviMate or IPTV Smarters from the Google Play Store and log in with your Xtream Codes details. On **Samsung (Tizen)** and **LG (webOS)**, look in the TV's own app store for an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U; availability changes by model year and region. If your TV has no suitable app, a Firestick or Android TV box is the simplest and most reliable fix.

## First: which kind of Smart TV do you have?

| Brand / system | How to tell | Best route |
|---|---|---|
| Android TV / Google TV (Sony, TCL, Philips, some Hisense, Xiaomi) | Home screen shows Google Play Store | Install a player from Google Play |
| Samsung (Tizen) | Samsung "Smart Hub" home bar | Samsung Apps store, or a streaming stick |
| LG (webOS) | LG "Content Store", ribbon-style home bar | LG Content Store, or a streaming stick |
| Hisense VIDAA, Roku TV, other systems | Own app store | Usually a streaming stick |

## What you need before you start

- Your login from the provider: **Xtream Codes** (server URL, username, password) or an **M3U playlist link**. Not sure which? See [Xtream Codes vs M3U](xtream-codes-vs-m3u.md).
- The TV connected to the internet, ideally with an Ethernet cable.
- The TV's software up to date (look for "Software update" in the TV's settings).

## Set up IPTV on an Android TV or Google TV

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on the TV.
2. Search for and install an IPTV player. **TiviMate** has the best TV guide layout; **IPTV Smarters Pro** is simpler and works on more platforms.
3. Open the app and choose **Xtream Codes** (in Smarters: *Login with Xtream Codes API*).
4. Enter the server URL, username and password exactly as your provider sent them. Include `http://` and the port number in the server URL if they're there.
5. Save and wait for the channels, movies and TV guide to finish loading. The first load can take a minute or two.
6. In the app's settings, turn on automatic **EPG (TV guide) updates**.

## Set up IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV (Tizen)

1. Press **Home** and open **Apps**.
2. Use the search icon to look for an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U. Read the description: some apps need a separate activation or only accept M3U.
3. Install it and open it. Many Samsung IPTV apps show a **device ID or MAC address** on first launch and ask you to add your playlist on the app's website. Follow the app's own instructions; never type your login into a site you reached from an unofficial link.
4. Add your Xtream Codes details or your M3U link.
5. Restart the app so the playlist loads.

If you can't find a suitable app for your model, skip to [the streaming stick option](#when-to-use-a-firestick-or-android-box-instead).

## Set up IPTV on an LG Smart TV (webOS)

1. Press **Home** and open the **LG Content Store** (called *Apps* on newer models).
2. Search for an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U.
3. Install it, open it, and note any device ID or MAC address it shows.
4. Add your playlist in the app, or on the app's official website if it asks you to.
5. Reopen the app and let the channel list load.

## When to use a Firestick or Android box instead

A streaming stick is often the better choice even on a Smart TV:

- **More apps:** the best IPTV players (such as TiviMate) are made for Android TV and Fire TV.
- **Faster:** a recent stick usually has a quicker processor than the TV's built-in system, so channels change faster and the TV guide loads more smoothly.
- **Updates:** Smart TV apps for IPTV come and go; Android apps are updated more often.

Setup takes about 15 minutes; see the [Firestick setup guide](README.md).

## Tips for smooth playback on a Smart TV

- Use Ethernet: most Smart TVs have a port, and it removes most buffering.
- Turn off the TV's motion smoothing for sport if the picture looks jerky.
- Name your playlist so you can find it later, and turn on automatic guide updates.
- If it buffers, follow the [buffering fix list](iptv-buffering-fix.md).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I watch IPTV on a Samsung Smart TV without a Firestick?**
Yes, if your model's app store has an IPTV player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U. Availability depends on the model year and region. If there isn't one, a Firestick or Android TV box is the simplest option.

**Which IPTV app is best for LG Smart TVs?**
Look in the LG Content Store for a player that supports Xtream Codes or M3U and has recent updates and reviews. If none works well on your model, use a streaming stick with TiviMate or IPTV Smarters.

**Is TiviMate available on Samsung or LG TVs?**
No. TiviMate is made for Android TV and Fire TV. On Samsung and LG, use a player from the TV's own store, or add a Firestick or Android box.

**Why does my Smart TV IPTV app ask for a MAC address?**
Some Smart TV players link your playlist to the TV's device ID or MAC address. You add your login on the app's official website, and the TV downloads it.

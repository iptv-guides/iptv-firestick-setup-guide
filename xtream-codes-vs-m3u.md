# Xtream Codes vs M3U: Which IPTV Login Type Should You Use? (2026)

> **Disclosure:** Written by the Ark IPTV team. Back to the [main setup guide](README.md).
>
> *Last updated: October 2026*

## The short answer
Use **Xtream Codes** when your provider gives you one. It loads the channels, movies, series and TV guide together. Use **M3U** when your app or device only accepts a playlist link.

## Comparison

| | Xtream Codes | M3U playlist |
|---|---|---|
| What you enter | Server URL, username, password | One playlist URL |
| TV guide (EPG) | Usually loaded automatically | May need a separate EPG link |
| Movies and series | Organized into sections | Often mixed into one big list |
| Updates | Refresh automatically | Reload the playlist when needed |
| Compatibility | Most dedicated IPTV apps | Almost every player, including VLC |

## How to choose
- **TiviMate, IPTV Smarters:** Xtream Codes.
- **VLC or a basic player:** M3U.
- **Very large playlist loads slowly:** try Xtream Codes.

## Common errors
- **Failed to authorize or login failed:** check for typos, extra spaces and the full server URL including `http://` and the port.
- **Empty list:** wait for the download to finish, then refresh.
- **Guide missing:** enable EPG update in the app settings.

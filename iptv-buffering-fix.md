# How to Fix IPTV Buffering and Freezing on Firestick and Smart TV (2026)

> **Disclosure:** Written by the Ark IPTV team. The fixes apply to any IPTV provider. Back to the [main setup guide](README.md).
>
> *Last updated: October 2026*

Buffering has a handful of causes. Check them in this order, from most to least common.

## 1. Test your real speed at the time it buffers
Run [speedtest.net](https://www.speedtest.net) on the same network and at the same time of day. Evening and weekend slowdowns often come from congestion in your area or too many devices at home.

| Stream | Stable speed to aim for |
|---|---|
| HD | 15 Mbps |
| Full HD / 4K | 25 Mbps or more |

## 2. Fix the connection
- Use Ethernet where possible (a Firestick needs an adapter).
- On Wi-Fi, use the 5 GHz band and keep the device close to the router.
- Pause large downloads, game updates and other streams.

## 3. Restart in the right order
1. Unplug the router for 30 seconds.
2. Restart the streaming device.
3. Reopen the IPTV app.

## 4. Clear the app and reload the playlist
- Clear the app cache (Settings > Applications > the app > Clear cache).
- Reload or re-add the playlist and let the TV guide finish downloading.

## 5. Check one channel or all channels
- **One channel only:** that stream is the problem, tell your provider the channel name and time.
- **Every channel:** the problem is your network, device or app.

## 6. Check the device
Older Firesticks have limited memory. Close other apps, free some storage and update the Fire OS.

## 7. Try a VPN test, not a VPN assumption
Some ISPs slow streaming traffic at peak hours. Test a channel with a VPN on and off at the same time of day. Keep whichever is smoother for you, and don't assume a VPN always helps.

## 8. Contact support with details
Send: device model, app name and version, channel, time, and the speed test result. That lets support find the cause quickly.
